# Instant inference credit: crypto payment addendum

Version: `instant-crypto-credit-2026-10-28-v1`

This addendum applies when you buy Instant inference credit with crypto through TMC Pay and this exact version is displayed and accepted before an invoice is created. It supplements the Instant inference credit terms (`instant-credit-2026-10-27-v1`). Where the two differ for a crypto purchase, this addendum applies. Everything else in those terms, including how credit is used, still applies.

## Your purchase

Instant is provided by RESI, INC. You choose a deposit from **US$5.00 to US$500.00**. Total deposits per account across all payment methods are limited to **US$1,000.00**. Each deposit creates one TMC Pay invoice in US dollars, payable in **USDC on the Base network**. A verified, fully paid invoice adds Instant inference credit **equal to the invoice amount**, once.

TMC Pay supplies the payment page, the exchange quote and a unique deposit address for each invoice. An invoice expires **30 minutes** after it is created. Send only USDC, only on Base, only to the address shown for your invoice, and only before it expires. Creating an invoice does not authorize a wallet transaction, subscription or automatic top-up.

Only one unfinished payment can exist on an account at a time, whether by card or crypto. Pay or let an open invoice expire before starting another one.

## Fees

Instant covers TMC Pay's payment-processing commission. **You pay any network (gas), wallet, exchange or withdrawal fee** charged to send your payment. Those fees do not purchase Instant credit and are not refunded by us. Check the full amount in your wallet before you approve a transfer.

## On-chain payments are irreversible

A blockchain transfer cannot be cancelled, reversed or charged back once it is broadcast. We cannot recover funds sent to the wrong address, on the wrong network, in an unsupported asset, or to an expired invoice. Recovery is not guaranteed even with our help. Never send a second payment to recover an earlier one.

Credit is added only after TMC Pay confirms the payment and we verify that the invoice belongs to your account and matches the amount you chose. A pending transaction, a wallet receipt, a payment-page redirect or a screenshot is not confirmation.

## Incomplete, excess and late payments

These are held for manual review and do not add credit automatically:

- an underpayment, or a payment that does not match the invoice amount;
- a payment that arrives after the invoice expired, or to a cancelled invoice;
- any other payment we cannot verify.

If a confirmed payment exceeds the invoice, we add only the invoice amount; the excess is reviewed separately. While a payment is under review, you cannot start another payment. Contact support with your invoice reference. Do not send another payment.

## Refunds

Except where law requires otherwise, purchases of credit are final and we do not offer voluntary refunds for a change of mind.

We provide refunds **only to correct billing errors** (such as a duplicate or excess payment, or credit we failed to deliver) **or where the law requires**. A refund covers at most the affected amount, excludes network fees, and is paid in USDC on Base to an address we verify as yours. An exchange deposit address is not a safe refund destination. We may ask you to prove control of the sending wallet.

## Your rights and support

Nothing in this addendum limits any nonwaivable consumer right.

For a payment problem, email [support@resilabs.ai](mailto:support@resilabs.ai) with your invoice reference and transaction hash. Never send seed phrases, private keys, passwords, API keys or private prompts.
